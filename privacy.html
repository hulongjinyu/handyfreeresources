Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Scenic Lens ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit our website.

We do not require you to create an account or provide personal information to browse our gallery. However, like most websites, we may automatically collect certain technical information, including browser type, operating system, referring website, pages visited and time spent, and approximate geographic location (country-level).

Cookies and Advertising

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on our website. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on our site. Google's use of the DoubleClick DART cookie enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

Third-party ad servers or ad networks may use technologies like cookies, JavaScript, or web beacons in their respective advertisements that appear on our site. We have no access to or control over these cookies used by third-party advertisers.

How We Use Information

GDPR (European Union)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have certain data protection rights under the GDPR. We do not sell your personal information. To exercise these rights, contact us at hello@handyfreeresources.com.

CCPA (California)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act, California residents have the right to know what personal information is collected, request deletion, and opt out of the sale of personal information. We do not sell your personal information.

Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
